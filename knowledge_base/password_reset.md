# Password reset emails

Article ID: KB-001
Support queue: account_access

## When to use this article
The customer requested a password reset email but has not received it.

## Customer troubleshooting steps
1. Check the Spam or Junk folder.
2. Confirm that the email address entered on the sign-in page is the
   address associated with the account. Do not post the address publicly.
3. If the email has not arrived after 10 minutes, the customer should
   request one new reset email using the sign-in page.
4. If the second email has not arrived after another 10 minutes,
   a human support agent should route the ticket to the account access team.

## Roles and capabilities
The customer requests reset emails through the sign-in page.
A human support agent handles escalation.
The AI assistant only drafts suggested replies.
It cannot request reset emails, access accounts, inspect delivery logs,
or escalate tickets.
No action has been performed merely because a draft was generated.

## Security rules
Never ask for passwords, reset links, or verification codes.

## Timing
The documented troubleshooting intervals are 10 minutes after each
request. No other delivery guarantees or waiting periods are specified.
