# Premium features unavailable after upgrading

Article ID: KB-002
Support queue: billing

## When to use this article
The customer upgraded their subscription but cannot access premium features.

## Troubleshooting steps
1. Sign out and sign back in to refresh the session.
2. Confirm that the customer is using the same account used for the upgrade.
3. Ask whether their billing page shows the upgrade as completed or pending.
4. If the upgrade shows as completed and signing in again does not
   restore access, escalate the ticket to the billing team.

## Security rules
Do not request card numbers, passwords, or payment authentication codes.

## Limits
The assistant cannot verify payment, change a subscription, or activate
premium features.
Do not promise an activation time or claim the issue has been fixed.
